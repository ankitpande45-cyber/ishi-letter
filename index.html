Dear Ishi,

I don't really know how to begin this, or whether I even deserve to write this to you, but there are some things sitting in my heart that I think you deserve to hear.

I'm genuinely sorry for everything that went wrong. For my words, my actions, and for the moments when I made you feel something you never deserved.

I know an apology cannot erase everything, and I know trust isn't something that can be rebuilt overnight.

Abhi turant toh nahi... but agar tum mujhe thoda time dedi na, toh shayad main jaisa ban kar rehna chahta hoon tumhari life mein, vaisa ban jaunga.

Someone who brings peace instead of confusion. Someone who understands instead of hurting. Someone who knows how to value your presence.

I'm not asking you to forget anything. I just wish I could have a little time to show you that I can do better.

I'm truly sorry, Ishi. ♡
